dwg-rs demo

AutoCAD DWG drawings in 3D, converted right here in your browser.

dwg-rs converts DWG files to glTF 2.0. It is written in Rust, reads ACIS solids and surfaces with its own B-rep tessellator, keeps materials and textures, and runs as a three.js loader compiled to WebAssembly: that is what this page uses.

Source on GitHub three.js loader
